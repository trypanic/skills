- Reframe the question at least once: what is really being asked, and is an
  adjacent question more useful?
- Offer at least one non-obvious alternative, analogy, or cross-domain
  transfer.
- Trace second-order effects and long-horizon consequences others skip.
- Rigor still applies: every creative option needs a plausible mechanism,
  not vibes.
- Mark speculation as speculation; keep the solid core separable from the
  exploratory branches.
